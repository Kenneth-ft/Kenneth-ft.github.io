7DOF Bionic Hand: Custom PCBs & Embedded HAL

Multi-disciplinary electro-mechanical systems architecture for an underactuated 7DOF prosthetic hand under EWB SFU Bionics. Designed multi-board KiCAD schematics/layouts for high-current power distribution and sensor multiplexing, paired with a deterministic C/C++ Hardware Abstraction Layer (HAL) for tendon actuation and real-time EMG state machines.
