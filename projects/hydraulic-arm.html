Hydraulic Robotic Manipulator

Articulated fluid-power robotic arm designed with custom mechanical advantage linkages, Autodesk Inventor CAD modeling, and 3D-printed structural components.

Role Project Lead & Mechanical Designer
Institution Capilano University • Engineering Design
CAD Tool Autodesk Inventor
Fabrication 3D Printing (PLA), Syringe Hydraulics

Project Overview

As project lead for our engineering design capstone at Capilano University, I led a student engineering team to design, calculate, and fabricate an articulated multi-axis hydraulic robotic arm. The design challenge required lifting specified payloads using closed fluid cylinders and mechanical linkages without electric motors.

Mechanical Design & Fluid Advantage

  • Mechanical Advantage Calculations: Calculated required piston surface area ratios and torque moments at each joint pivot to lift maximum target payloads under Pascal's Principle (\(F_2 = F_1 \times \frac{A_2}{A_1}\)).
  • Linkage Optimization: Modeled 3D kinematic linkages in Autodesk Inventor, ensuring full range of angular motion without linkage binding or cylinder rod buckling.
  • Rapid Prototyping: Optimized print orientations, wall count, and infill percentages in Cura for 3D-printed structural brackets to resist high shearing stresses at pivot pins.

Project Management & Team Leadership

Organized and scheduled team tasks based on continuity requirements by establishing a detailed Gantt chart and burn-down tracking system. Ensured milestones were hit ahead of schedule and led technical report writing and final live physical demonstration.
