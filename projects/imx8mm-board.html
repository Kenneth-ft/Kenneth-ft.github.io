i.MX8MM Processor Expansion PCB

Custom expansion board designed in KiCAD interfacing SPI and UART high-speed buses directly to an NXP i.MX8M Mini application processor for industrial water telemetry.

Company Promag Enviro Systems
Role Hardware & Firmware Developer
EDA Software KiCAD
Bus Protocols SPI, UART, I2C, RS-485

Project Overview

At Promag Enviro Systems, the primary industrial controller platform relies on an NXP i.MX8M Mini quad-core ARM processor. To interface the main processor with specialized water-quality sensors and telemetry radios, I designed, simulated, and specified components for a custom daughterboard PCB in KiCAD.

Circuit Design & Signal Integrity

  • High-Speed Bus Interfacing: Routed SPI and UART traces with matched length and controlled impedance lines to prevent signal reflections and cross-talk during high-speed data transmission.
  • Transient Voltage & ESD Protection: Implemented TVS diode arrays and galvanic optoisolators on external sensor lines to protect the processor against industrial ground loops and inductive surges.
  • Component Selection & BOM Management: Sourced active components, DC-DC step-down buck regulators, and level-shifters to translate between 1.8V processor I/O logic and 3.3V/5.0V peripheral buses.

Hardware Bring-Up & Verification

Conducted complete benchtop hardware bring-up in the lab:

  1. Power rail verification: validated voltage sequencing and ripple under maximum current load using a digital multimeter and oscilloscope.
  2. Signal analysis: decoded SPI clock and MISO/MOSI packets using a digital logic analyzer to verify timing margins and rise times.
  3. Connected the board to Linux kernel device trees to confirm hardware recognition.
