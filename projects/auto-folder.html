Optoelectronic Automated Folder

Automated mechatronic folding system utilizing AutoCAD technical drawings, an Arduino microcontroller, I2C sensor bus, LDR light sensors, and multi-servo actuation.

Role Project Lead & Systems Developer
Institution Capilano University • Engineering Design
Microcontroller Arduino / ATmega328P
Sensors & Actuation LDRs, Limit Switches, PWM Servos, I2C Bus

Project Overview

Designed and built an automated mechatronic folding machine designed to standardize and speed up garment handling. The machine automatically detects the presence and placement of material using optoelectronic light sensors, executing a synchronized folding cycle through an array of motorized folding paddles.

Electrical & Sensor Integration

  • Optoelectronic Detection: Array of ambient light-dependent resistors (LDRs) and momentary limit switches connected across an I2C communication bus to detect item boundary placement without physical interference.
  • Multi-Servo Power Stage: Designed an independent external 5V regulator power bus to prevent servo inrush currents from resetting the microcontroller logic.
  • Schematics & Wiring: Drafted complete wiring harness schematics and a detailed Bill of Materials (BOM) in AutoCAD.

Mechanical Paddle Kinematics

Engineered sequential folding panels actuated by high-torque servos. Formulated a weighted design selection matrix to evaluate panel materials (acrylic vs. corrugated composite) based on rigidity, weight, and friction coefficients against fabric.
