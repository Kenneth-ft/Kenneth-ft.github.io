6DOF Articulated Robot Arm

Six-axis robotic manipulator engineered with custom structural joint linkages, stepper motor actuation, closed-loop hall-effect encoder feedback, and C++ inverse kinematics.

Role Lead Designer & Firmware Developer
Project Type Advanced Robotics Engineering
Timeline 2025 – Present
Disciplines Kinematics, CAD / SolidWorks, Closed-Loop Control
Tools SolidWorks, C++, MATLAB, Stepper Drivers, Hall Encoders

Project Overview

This project focuses on the ground-up mechanical design and embedded control of a 6-degree-of-freedom (6DOF) articulated serial robotic manipulator. Unlike hobbyist servo arms that lack rigidity and position feedback, this manipulator is engineered with planetary gearbox reduction, NEMA stepper motors, and magnetic hall-effect absolute encoders on each joint for closed-loop motion control.

Kinematic Modeling & Mathematical Analysis

Building on technical coursework in Advanced Kinematics for Robotic Systems, the manipulator's geometry was modeled using standardized robotic conventions:

  • Denavit-Hartenberg (DH) Parameters: Formulated DH parameter frames for all 6 joints to calculate the forward kinematic homogeneous transformation matrices \($T_0^6$\).
  • Inverse Kinematics Solver: Implemented analytical and numerical inverse kinematics in C++ to translate arbitrary Cartesian coordinate goals \((X, Y, Z, \text{Roll}, \text{Pitch}, \text{Yaw})\) into corresponding joint angles \(\theta_1\) through \(\theta_6\).
  • Singularity & Workspace Analysis: Simulated boundary conditions in MATLAB to avoid wrist and shoulder kinematic singularities where joint velocities diverge.

Mechanical Design & Bearing Integration

Leveraging four years of mechanical bearing overhaul and bicycle diagnostics:

  • Thrust & Radial Bearing Preloading: Designed custom dual angular-contact bearing housings for the shoulder and elbow joints to absorb high moment loads without joint deflection.
  • Torsional Stiffness: Reinforced 3D printed PETG/PLA structural shells with aluminum standoffs to ensure zero backlash under high accelerations.
  • Modular End-Effector: Designed a quick-change mechanical flange allowing interchangeable mechanical grippers, vacuum suction cups, or pen plotting attachments.
