Industrial Telemetry Enclosure

Waterproof, ruggedized IP-rated enclosure engineered in Fusion 360 for an industrial environmental monitoring telemetry controller at Promag Enviro Systems.

Company Promag Enviro Systems
Role Product Development Team
CAD Tool Autodesk Fusion 360
Manufacturing DFM, Injection Molding & CNC Machining

Project Overview

During my product development role at Promag Enviro Systems, I was tasked with engineering a production-ready waterproof enclosure to house high-value environmental telemetry and chemical monitoring controllers operating in corrosive outdoor wastewater plants.

Mechanical Engineering & DFM

  • O-Ring & Gasket Compression: Modeled continuous silicone perimeter sealing grooves in Fusion 360, performing cross-sectional compression calculations to ensure IP67 environmental sealing under thermal cycling.
  • Design for Manufacturability (DFM): Formulated proper draft angles (1.5° – 2°), uniform wall thicknesses to prevent sink marks, and rib structures to withstand high impact shock loads during field transport.
  • Vendor Interfacing & GD&T: Produced detailed technical 2D prints specifying tolerances (ASME Y14.5 GD&T), communicating directly with injection molding and CNC machining manufacturers to validate mold flow and tooling clearances.

Thermal & Cable Gland Management

Engineered modular cable gland bulkheads for waterproof sensor cable entry (PG7 and PG9 glands) and integrated an internal aluminum conduction heat spreader to sink heat generated by the on-board power management ICs away from internal air cavity into external heatsink fins.
